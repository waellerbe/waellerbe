# Amway B2B Outreach Plan
### Prince William County, VA — Woodbridge & Manassas Focus

---

## Strategic Foundation

The goal is to identify local businesses where Amway's product lines solve a real, existing need — not to convince anyone they have a problem. Each outreach is framed around the business owner's current customers, staff wellness, or operational costs.

**Two cities. Two dynamics.**

- **Woodbridge** — high-volume commercial corridor (Route 1, Potomac Mills area). More businesses, faster cycles, relationship built through repeat visibility.
- **Manassas** — tighter small-business community, active downtown, easier to become a trusted face. Historic district + growing healthcare cluster.

## Four-Phase Outreach Cadence

### Phase 1 — Research & Target Mapping (Week 1–2)
- Build a list of 20–30 businesses per city using Google Maps, Yelp, and the [Prince William Chamber of Commerce Directory](https://www.pwchamber.org)
- For each business, identify: owner name, best time to visit, current product/supplement habits if visible online
- Score each prospect: High / Medium / Low based on product-line fit

### Phase 2 — Warm Entry via Community (Week 2–4)
- Join the **Prince William Chamber of Commerce** — attends monthly mixers, gets into the member directory
- Locate and attend a **BNI Chapter** (Business Network International) — both Woodbridge and Manassas have active chapters; this is a structured referral network
- At every event: introduce yourself as a *wellness and business solutions consultant*, not as a distributor
- Goal: 5 warm introductions per week, not sales pitches

### Phase 3 — Direct Business Visits (Week 3–6)
- Visit during off-peak hours: Tuesday–Thursday, 10am–12pm or 2pm–4pm
- Always ask for the owner, not staff
- Lead with curiosity, not a catalog — use the conversation starters above
- Leave behind: one clean, single-page overview (not a full product catalog)
- Log every visit: date, contact name, reaction, follow-up date

**Follow-up Rule:** Contact within 5 business days. Three touchpoints max before moving on.

### Phase 4 — Conversion & Long-Term Retention
- Offer a personal sample or trial kit to the owner first — they need to be a believer
- Once the owner experiences results, introduce the B2B supply conversation naturally
- Set up Amway's auto-delivery (recurring order) to reduce friction
- Check in monthly — not to sell, but to support

## Outreach Scripts

### Cold Visit — Gym / Fitness Studio
> *"Hi, I'm [Name] — I work with local fitness businesses in Prince William County. I represent Nutrilite, which is actually the world's #1 selling supplement brand. I'm not here to pitch anything today — I just like connecting with owners who care about what they recommend to their members. Do you have 5 minutes to grab a card and chat?"*

---

### Cold Visit — Salon / Spa
> *"Hi, I'm [Name] — I partner with salons in the area who want to offer their clients a premium skincare line without the overhead of a full retail setup. I work with Artistry by Amway — it's a clinical-grade brand that's been around for 60 years. Would you be open to a quick conversation about how other salons in the area are adding it to their service menu?"*

---

### Cold Visit — Chiropractor / PT Clinic
> *"Hi, I'm [Name] — I work with wellness practitioners who want a clean supplement option to recommend to patients. I represent Nutrilite, which is NSF certified and has a 90-year science background. A lot of practitioners appreciate having something they can stand behind. Is the doctor or practice owner available for just a few minutes?"*

---

### Cold Visit — Small Office
> *"Hi, I'm [Name] — I help small businesses in Prince William County set up low-cost wellness programs for their teams. Things like a monthly vitamin subscription or clean office supplies. It's something a lot of office managers appreciate because it's a perk that doesn't break the budget. Would your HR or office manager have 10 minutes to hear about it?"*
